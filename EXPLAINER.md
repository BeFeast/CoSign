# What is CoSign? (pitch cheat-sheet)

## The one-liner
**CoSign is a shared receipt for who made what on a track** — and it locks the splits so they can't quietly change.

## 5-second version (pick one)
- "It's the **cap table for a song**."
- "**Splitwise for royalties** — who owns what, agreed and locked."
- "A **paper trail** for beats/samples so nobody 'forgets' the split later."

## 15-second version (say it out loud)
> When you make samples, beats and songs with other people, who owns what gets messy fast — a 50/50 agreed in a DM quietly becomes 70/30, and by the time a song needs clearing nobody remembers who was on the sample. CoSign writes it down and **locks it**: splits only change when **every person on the work agrees**. Then it hands you a clean credit + PRO sheet to send.

## The problem it kills
1. Splits agreed in DMs and forgotten.
2. "Wait, did he make the sample, or just a beat with it?" (layers blur).
3. A label asks for everyone's PRO/IPI → scavenger hunt across chats.

## What it actually does
- **Logs who made which layer** (sample → beat → song, with real lineage).
- **Locks ownership** — any split change needs everyone to approve (no silent edits).
- **Outputs a clearance-ready credit + PRO pack** as one shareable link.

## Where it's going (the wedge)
Producers already name files like `Velvet 95bpm Gm @antn42 @timmyxholliday`.
CoSign will **read that filename** → auto-fill the work + who's on it. Zero admin — it reads work you already do.

---
*Not a marketplace (that's BeatStars) and not a distributor. It's the layer before and after: provenance + agreement + clean handoff.*
